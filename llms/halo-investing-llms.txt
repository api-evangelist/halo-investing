# Halo Investing

> Halo Investing is a Chicago-based financial technology company that operates a marketplace for "protective investments" — structured notes, multi-manager structured note SMAs, buffered ETFs, annuities and insurance — connecting financial advisors, RIAs and financial institutions with more than 40 global banking and insurance issuers.

Founded in 2015 by Biju Kulathakal and Jason Barsema, Halo lets advisors analyze, customize, price, execute and then monitor outcome-based investment products from a single dashboard, including tracking observation and maturity dates on issued notes. The company reports more than $45.5B in transactions since 2015 and employs 100+ people. Its affiliate Halo Securities LLC is an SEC-registered broker-dealer and a member of FINRA/SIPC, acting as distributor and selling agent for structured note products.

Two catch-all traps on this domain produce false positives and are documented in the well-known probe record: the WordPress marketing site returns HTTP 200 with an HTML article for **every** unknown path (so `/llms.txt` and `/openapi.json` "200s" are soft-404s), and `*.haloinvesting.com` is a wildcard DNS record, so `api.haloinvesting.com` resolves and answers 502 despite not being an API host.
